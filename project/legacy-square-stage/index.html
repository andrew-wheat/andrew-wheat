Legacy Square Stage

Professional Work, Mithun
Renton, Washington
Supervised by Richard Franko
Worked with Chris Lim, Marna Abrams, and Samantha Sweeney
Client: City of Renton
Project team: Mithun, KPFF, LSTN Consultants, P2S, HLB Lighting Design, and Andersen Construction

Role: Diagram planning and creation; marketing materials
Project: 900 sq ft civic performance stage, completed 2026

Legacy Square Stage is a civic performance shell in downtown Renton, designed for public events and everyday use. Working with Mithun's project team, I planned and created the diagrams and marketing materials shown here to communicate its urban setting, public-space relationships, stage plan, park section, and operating modes.
